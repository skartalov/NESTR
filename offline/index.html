Allowance: minimum space between pieces and the sheet border (right and top). A gap smaller than this is not left: the piece goes elsewhere or onto a new sheet. A gap of exactly 0 (pieces fill the sheet length) is always allowed.
Gap: uncut distance at both ends of every cut, where it meets the sheet border or a crossing cut (G-code only).
